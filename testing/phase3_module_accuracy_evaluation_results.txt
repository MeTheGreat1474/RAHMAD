PART 4: ACADEMIC DISCUSSION FOR FYP THESIS EVALUATION CHAPTER
================================================================================
1. Why Early Predictor Alone Is Insufficient:
   While the CLIP+LoRA Early Predictor exhibits high speed (~few milliseconds) and decent accuracy,
   it operates primarily on surface linguistic cues, embedding alignment, and lexical tokens. It
   inevitably produces false positives on breaking real news with unusual prose, and false negatives on
   subtly fabricated stories that ape professional journalistic style.

2. The Grounding Impact of Knowledge Retrieval (KR):
   Adding KR produces an immediate jump in factual recall and precision. By dynamically retrieving canonical
   reporting from whitelisted publishers and cross-referencing named entities and figures, the Judge can
   conclusively confirm when an event actually occurred in reality.

3. Multi-Agent Debate (MAD) Exposes Covert Framing & Discrepancies:
   The Multi-Agent Debate introduces adversarial scrutiny: debaters assigned to the skeptical side actively
   probe the text for selective quoting, unverified numbers, and lack of attribution. This clash
   supplies the Judge with concrete arguments rather than passive facts, enabling the detection of nuanced
   disinformation categories (e.g. MISLEADING CONTENT, FALSE CONNECTION).

4. Multimodal Synergy in the Full Pipeline:
   The combination of all 6 modules creates a defense-in-depth architecture where no single failure compromises
   the verdict. The system achieves high accuracy with calibrated confidence, providing human-readable
   forensic justifications suitable for deployment in critical media intelligence.

5. Text-Visual Independence & Resolving Cheapfakes (The Config 6 vs Config 7 Phenomenon):
   In multimodal misinformation, genuine unmanipulated photographs are frequently paired with fabricated,
   speculative, or unverified captions (Cheapfakes / False Connection). When Reverse Image Search confirms that
   the photograph is authentic, LLM judges can be vulnerable to a 'visual halo effect' if an authentic photo
   is conflated with overall article credibility. RAHMAD resolves this through Constitutional Principle P8,
   explicit Image-to-Text Entailment verification, and MAD skeptical debater scrutiny, ensuring that an authentic
   photo never excuses uncorroborated or misleading textual assertions.
